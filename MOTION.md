# Motion ownership and behavior

Timings live in `src/motion.js`. `scene.js` owns all 3D transforms; `main.js` owns scroll/navigation and HTML transitions. CSS owns checkpoint opacity/translation. No competing tween systems.

- States: idle → dragging → settling → spinning → playing. Invalid drop or Escape: dragging → returning → idle. Plain clicks select without extracting. A 5px pointer threshold separates click and drag. Busy states reject duplicate placements; re-grabbing a returning disc continues from its current pose.
- Drag: captured pointer, stable camera-facing plane, preserved grab offset, immediate position updates. The disc lifts and turns flat over 180ms. Generous screen-projected platter ellipse; warm halo indicates a valid drop. Release outside or Escape returns over 430ms. No sleeve is removed permanently.
- Placement: center/lower over 620ms, smoothly accelerate toward 33⅓ rpm over 900ms, pivot the tonearm. Introduction playback lasts 2500ms before handoff; section acknowledgement lasts 700ms. No audio.
- Introduction: one 1250ms smoothstep automatic scroll, cancelled by wheel, touch, pointer press, or navigation keys. Input during playback also prevents a later automatic scroll. It never restarts on its own. Then native scroll grows the central line and reveals checkpoints; revealed blocks remain in document flow and stay readable on return.
- Section navigation: 480ms shared-art transition into an HTML header. Direct URLs and Index can bypass placement. Route tokens suppress obsolete completion/focus callbacks. Return restores saved scroll/selection and resets the final disc/arm.
- Lighting: the old trigger was 74% of the former pinned 340vh scroll travel; there was no fixed millisecond fade duration. Its replacement is entry into the final scene’s reveal zone: its top crosses above 78% of viewport height, its bottom remains below 12%, and its canvas intersects the viewport. Start is delayed by **500ms**, then a separately defined **900ms** fade runs. The delay does not lengthen that fade. Exit, page hiding, and navigation cancel pending/running reveals. Back restoration makes the already-visited collection immediately ready.
- Reduced motion: 100ms settle, 140ms acknowledgement, no rotation, direct introduction jump, no shared-art animation, static readable checkpoints, and no cursor-light tracking. The lighting delay remains; its fade is skipped.
- Hover uses exponential damping at rate 12 and stationary proxies, so rapid selection redirects rather than queues. Lighting movement is bounded and subdued during playback.
- Rendering: DPR ≤1.5, 800px sleeves, one 1024px shadow light per scene, no postprocessing. Render only during change or visible playback. Offscreen, hidden-tab, and detail-page scenes stop; interrupted placements reset when leaving a scene. CPU submission metrics are diagnostic, not FPS guarantees.
