# Motion ownership and behavior

Timings live in `src/motion.js`. `scene.js` owns all 3D transforms; `main.js` owns navigation and HTML transitions. CSS owns the selected-summary reveal. No competing tween systems.

- States: idle → dragging/settling → closing → spinning → playing. Invalid drop or Escape: dragging → returning → idle. Hover or focus updates the HTML summary; a single activation begins placement. A 5px pointer threshold separates click and drag. Busy states reject duplicate placements; interrupted transforms continue from their current pose.
- Drag: captured pointer, stable camera-facing plane, preserved grab offset, immediate position updates. The disc lifts and turns flat over 230ms. Generous screen-projected platter ellipse; warm halo indicates a valid drop. Release outside or Escape returns over 630ms. No sleeve is removed permanently.
- Placement: center/lower over 750ms, close the hinged acrylic lid over 690ms, then smoothly accelerate toward 33⅓ rpm over 930ms, pivot the tonearm and lower it over 930ms after a 290ms approach. Section acknowledgement lasts 1580ms. No audio.
- Section navigation: 605ms shared-art transition into an HTML header. Direct URLs and Index can bypass placement. Route tokens suppress obsolete completion/focus callbacks. Return restores saved scroll/selection and resets the final disc/arm.
- Lighting: the old trigger was 74% of the former pinned 340vh scroll travel; there was no fixed millisecond fade duration. Its replacement is entry into the final scene’s reveal zone: its top crosses above 78% of viewport height, its bottom remains below 12%, and its canvas intersects the viewport. Start is delayed by **500ms**, then a separately defined **1295ms** fade runs. The delay does not lengthen that fade. Exit, page hiding, and navigation cancel pending/running reveals. Back restoration makes the already-visited collection immediately ready.
- Reduced motion: 100ms settle, 140ms acknowledgement, no rotation, no shared-art animation, static readable biography, and no cursor-light tracking. The lighting delay remains; its fade is skipped.
- Hover lifts a sleeve by 0.42 scene units and updates its HTML summary. Stable original-position proxies remain the primary hit targets. Selection uses exponential damping at rate 10.5 and stationary proxies, so rapid input redirects rather than queues. Lighting movement is bounded and subdued during playback.
- Rendering: DPR ≤1.5, 800px sleeves, one 1024px shadow light, no postprocessing. Render only during change or visible playback. Offscreen, hidden-tab, and detail-page scenes stop; interrupted placements reset when leaving the scene. CPU submission metrics are diagnostic, not FPS guarantees.
- Startup: paint the biography first, then asynchronously build the collection. Material generation yields between strips; rigid opaque meshes are batched inside their transform owners. Textures and both scene/shadow shaders warm before interaction. The 64px reflection capture keeps broad studio highlights. A warm render uses the actual canvas format to avoid recompiling a second output variant. The canvas stays hidden until ready; HTML scrolling/navigation remain live throughout.
