# Motion ownership and behavior

Timings live in `src/motion.js`. `scene.js` owns all 3D transforms; `main.js` owns scroll/navigation and HTML transitions. CSS owns checkpoint opacity/translation. No competing tween systems.

- States: idle → dragging/settling → spinning → playing. Invalid drop or Escape: dragging → returning → idle. A single click selects and updates the HTML summary; a second click on the same sleeve within 420ms begins placement. A 5px pointer threshold separates click and drag. Busy states reject duplicate placements; interrupted transforms continue from their current pose.
- Drag: captured pointer, stable camera-facing plane, preserved grab offset, immediate position updates. The disc lifts and turns flat over 180ms. Generous screen-projected platter ellipse; warm halo indicates a valid drop. Release outside or Escape returns over 560ms. No sleeve is removed permanently.
- Placement: center/lower over 760ms, smoothly accelerate toward 33⅓ rpm over 900ms, pivot the tonearm and lower it over 650ms after a 200ms approach. Section acknowledgement lasts 1200ms. No audio.
- Introduction: three viewport-height checkpoints remain in normal document flow before the collection. Native scroll drives a restrained 24px rise and opacity reveal across 46% of viewport height. Pausing or reversing scroll pauses or reverses the reveal naturally; completed checkpoints stay readable on return.
- Section navigation: 480ms shared-art transition into an HTML header. Direct URLs and Index can bypass placement. Route tokens suppress obsolete completion/focus callbacks. Return restores saved scroll/selection and resets the final disc/arm.
- Lighting: the old trigger was 74% of the former pinned 340vh scroll travel; there was no fixed millisecond fade duration. Its replacement is entry into the final scene’s reveal zone: its top crosses above 78% of viewport height, its bottom remains below 12%, and its canvas intersects the viewport. Start is delayed by **500ms**, then a separately defined **900ms** fade runs. The delay does not lengthen that fade. Exit, page hiding, and navigation cancel pending/running reveals. Back restoration makes the already-visited collection immediately ready.
- Reduced motion: 100ms settle, 140ms acknowledgement, no rotation, no shared-art animation, static readable checkpoints, and no cursor-light tracking. The lighting delay remains; its fade is skipped.
- Selection uses exponential damping at rate 12 and stationary proxies, so rapid input redirects rather than queues. Lighting movement is bounded and subdued during playback.
- Rendering: DPR ≤1.5, 800px sleeves, one 1024px shadow light, no postprocessing. Render only during change or visible playback. Offscreen, hidden-tab, and detail-page scenes stop; interrupted placements reset when leaving the scene. CPU submission metrics are diagnostic, not FPS guarantees.
