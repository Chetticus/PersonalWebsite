# Art direction

A personal listening ritual: lift a record, place it, let it turn, then follow the story. The physical objects carry the atmosphere; surrounding HTML stays quiet.

- Palette: espresso brown `#17120f`, warm ivory `#e8dfd0`, muted text `#a8a699`, warm brass `#c9b383`, walnut `#493628`, charcoal `#222723`. Eight existing sleeve accents remain distinct.
- Type: Georgia for personal story headings; Arial for body text and controls. Generous line height, short paragraphs, no decorative numbering or top navigation bar.
- Opening: the introduction leads immediately with the portrait, name, and short background. A quiet central line connects the left/right/left checkpoints in document flow. Earlier content stays readable and the line ends before the collection.
- Collection: one enlarged turntable and crate share the frame. The first click raises a sleeve and reveals its title/copy below; double-click or drag moves its disc to the platter. Index, résumé, Play, and motion controls are discreet.
- Materials: rounded walnut veneer plinth with original 512px grain/bump maps; inset brushed gunmetal deck; polished, grooved platter edge with instanced strobe dots; concentric rubber mat; layered isolation feet; recessed controls and fasteners. The articulated tonearm remains; dust covers and hinges are removed from both models. A prefiltered studio reflection environment reveals metal edges; its intensity follows scene illumination. Broad warm light and ambient fill retain the intimate setting.
- Portrait: no actual photo supplied. The labeled monogram is replaceable through `identity.js`. HTML relighting is a restrained overlay; accurate facial relighting needs a matching depth asset and remains unimplemented.
- References: tkex informs turntable form/components, implemented originally due to absent reuse terms; cratedigger supplies our existing crate foundation; RelightingImages informs light; Palmer informs art continuity; Blue Note informs original sleeve composition. No room, physics toys, reference audio, or external model assets are included.

- Shared surface: a dark walnut desk receives the objects’ shadows and fades at its perimeter, keeping the composition quiet. The exposed turntable is scaled to 0.935 and shifted left to x=-3.8; movable discs match its scale.
- Sleeve identity: eight original compositions use editorial typography, a record cutout, paired letterforms, community connections, blueprint lines, research orbits, stepped forms, a globe, and topographic paths. All section titles remain clear; these are conceptual illustrations, not claimed project evidence.
- Content pages inherit the sleeve accent, with a restrained header wash, sticky story navigation, résumé summaries, clearly labeled reflection prompts, and an honest media placeholder. No invented quotations, photos, demonstrations, or outcomes.

- The opening has no top bar, gate, or placement prompt. Its hierarchy stays focused on the portrait and introduction.
