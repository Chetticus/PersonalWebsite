# Art direction

A personal listening ritual: lift a record, place it, let it turn, then follow the story. The physical objects carry the atmosphere; surrounding HTML stays quiet.

- Palette: listening-room black `#080908`, paper `#e9e4d9`, muted text `#a8a699`, warm brass `#c9b383`, walnut `#493628`, charcoal `#222723`. Eight existing sleeve accents remain distinct.
- Type: Georgia for the name and personal story headings; Arial for body text and controls. Generous line height, short paragraphs, no decorative numbering or top navigation bar.
- Opening: a recognizable turntable at left, portrait sleeve prominently forward at right, other records subdued behind it. One placement instruction. The square sleeve stays outside the platter; only the circular disc moves and spins.
- Introduction: quiet central line, restrained markers, and left/right/left checkpoints in document flow. Earlier content stays readable. The line ends before the collection.
- Collection: same turntable design at left and a larger, thin-sleeved wood crate at right. Selected title/copy and a placement action sit below. Index, résumé, and motion controls are discreet.
- Materials: matte sleeves, dark grooved vinyl, walnut sides, charcoal plinth, brushed metal platter, raised translucent dust cover, and an articulated tonearm. Broad warm light, faint ambient fill, and a destination light keep the platter discoverable during dragging.
- Portrait: no actual photo supplied. The labeled monogram is replaceable through `identity.js`. HTML relighting is a restrained overlay; accurate facial relighting needs a matching depth asset and remains unimplemented.
- References: tkex informs turntable form/components, implemented originally due to absent reuse terms; cratedigger supplies our existing crate foundation; RelightingImages informs light; Palmer informs art continuity; Blue Note informs original sleeve composition. No room, physics toys, reference audio, or external model assets are included.
